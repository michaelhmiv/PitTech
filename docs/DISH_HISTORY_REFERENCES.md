# Dish history and comparison

Each loaded dish exposes Previous cook beside This cook. The version used in that meal is the initial historical baseline; switching the displayed version does not alter the meal's source binding. Saved observations, quantities, measured durations, temperatures, outcomes and independently owned photos remain available after deletion of the source meal. Missing ratings are left absent.

Original UTC timestamps and zones are shown. Relative offsets use the recorded historical Food on event; preparation may have negative offsets. Missing anchors remain unavailable. Heating intervals retain removal/return cycles and leave an unrecorded end open. Changing editable instructions never rewrites recorded observations.

Android and native charts reuse stable channel identity, explicit recorded anchors, unit conversion and gap segmentation. Dish-private channels and notes are filtered by ownership. Shared pit channels are eligible only without another dish's assignment. Historical and current targets and actions remain independent. Source annotations open the original meal context. Source deletion disables full-curve comparison while leaving frozen facts usable.

Validation includes historical projection and archive round trips on both platforms, existing chart unit/gap/identity coverage, scope extraction tests, and the required emulator/native build gates. Physical controller tests are separate.
