# Preparation and equipment

Saved ingredient combos live in the dish's existing preparation details. Applying a combo copies ingredients into the dish; later edits never change the saved recipe. Optional recipe yield enables quantity scaling only when every ingredient has a measured compatible unit and the requested yield uses the same unit. Cooking times and temperatures never scale.

The existing cook-tools section opens a per-cook checklist built from ingredient quantities, fuel, supplies and preparation notes. Like ingredient quantities combine only when the ingredient, brand and unit match. Unmeasured or incompatible quantities remain separate. The list supports checking, editing, removing and adding items; checking a supply creates no physical cooking event.

Devices → My equipment contains smoker profiles, default fuel/blend, recorded cooking hours, user-selected cleaning intervals and maintenance resets. Cook setup explicitly offers profiles and remains editable. Equipment names link to recorded smoker names and are stable once saved.

Recorded hours use the union of actual Food on through removal/rest/hold/done intervals, counting simultaneous dishes once. Setup and rest do not add smoker hours. Refills and explicitly measured fuel use are separate records. A range appears only after two completed cooks with actual cooking intervals and measured fuel use; no empty-hopper or exact burn-rate claim is made.

All records are included in full ZIP backups and the existing saved-guidance CSV/workbook tables. Restoring histories does not resume controller recording, guidance or alerts. Tests exercise compatible quantities, yield validation, overlapping cooking intervals, maintenance resets, measured fuel ranges, portable restoration and access through the existing Devices and Live screens at both CI font scales.
