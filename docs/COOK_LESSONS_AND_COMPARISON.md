# Learn from a cook

Results offers optional Keep doing and Change next time notes alongside existing ratings. Cook again/playbook previews surface those lessons. Favorites, method and tags stay lightweight cook metadata. Insights keeps its search and adds Favorites/4+ chips and smoker selection; search includes cut, size, ingredients/rubs, method and tags.

Charts offers one reference per current dish, with an explicit reference dish and channel mapping. Compare food to food or pit to pit, align from actual Food on or Wrap, normalize units and preserve the existing chart's gaps and quality filtering. Milestone timing is shown alongside notes with a direct link to the reference cook's underlying timeline/photos. Reference deletion is handled by selecting another cook rather than fabricating missing history.

Saved playbook revisions show linked completed outcomes. Active plans remain snapshots: editing a playbook or notes does not mutate an ongoing cook. Favorites, lessons, reference choices and tags are included in ordinary local archives. Comparisons describe evidence without causal or exact-ETA claims.
