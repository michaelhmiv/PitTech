# Native share cards

Android and iOS render a 1080 × 1350 PNG from the same versioned presentation
contract and fixture. Native font rasterization can differ. The fixed export
layout has a chosen chart/photo hero, selected dish names, up to five recorded
milestones, up to three known stats, an optional card note and the PitTech footer.
Absent data collapses its slots. The original measurements and full text stay
in the Cook Pack; image text alone is shortened.

Share meal and Share recipe both create an independently owned sharing draft.
Story and photo inclusion default off. A recipe draft pins its selected revision
and copies its available photos without selecting them. The card note is a
separate, explicit field; private source descriptions and result notes are never
automatically used as a quotation. The verdict option uses recorded story
results, not edited recipe ratings. The card remains free without an account.

The Chart option uses only enabled public story projections. Channels retain
logical probe identity even when labels match, and each dish retains its own
Food on anchor. Invalid readings, manual samples, explicit connection gaps and
cadence breaks remain separate segments. Each segment is reduced to at most
200 extrema-preserving points. Original precise readings remain in the pack.
The renderer labels a single chosen display unit and retains negative offsets.
No source measurements means no chart hero. No duration, weight or verdict is
invented. Photo crop/zoom is explicit and uses an orientation-correct,
location-free derivative; source originals remain unchanged.

The preview shows the exact PNG, with system sharing, Save PNG, retry and
Cook Pack fallback. Canceling keeps the saved draft. iOS shares the rendered
image in memory. Android grants the receiver read access to a private cache file
for asynchronous reads, prunes files older than 24 hours and retains at most 12;
pruning runs on creation and app start. Preview cancellation creates no file.

Relay code/link is a separate renderer input supplied only after a successful
upload. The draft has no editable receipt/code field. The current local flow
never supplies a receipt; the fixture exercises the future receipt layout.
Only the classic free theme is accepted; future themes need an explicit contract
and entitlement change.

Actions runs the actual bitmap renderers against identical chart/photo/sparse/
long-Unicode/relay fixtures and retains the exported PNGs, plus accessible
preview/cancel-share UI evidence on both platforms. A passing dimension check
alone does not complete pixel review. See STATUS.md for the actual gate state.
